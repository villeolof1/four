FOUR — Credits & source

A Connect Four game by Ville Wedenberg. Easy, Medium and Hard use his own neural-network models trained through self-play. Pro uses an independent solver; it is not one of those trained models.

Pro solver: Connect Four AI by Benjamin Rall, MIT. Neural-network inference: ONNX Runtime by Microsoft, MIT. Fonts: DM Sans and Manrope, served by Google Fonts.
